GariHakikaBot

We run a crawler that reads publicly available vehicle listings to build a price index for Kenyan car buyers. This page explains exactly what it does, and gives you a one-email way to stop it.

Want us to stop? Email data@garihakika.co.ke with your domain. We remove it within 48 hours, permanently, and we keep a record. You do not need to explain why.

How to identify us

Our requests carry this user agent:

GariHakikaBot/1.0 (+https://garihakika.co.ke/bot; contact: data@garihakika.co.ke)

If a request claims to be us and does not match that string, it is not us.

What we collect

  • Vehicle facts: make, model, year, price, mileage, engine size, fuel, transmission, body type, town
  • The public URL of the listing, so we can link buyers back to you
  • The dealer’s business name and yard location

What we refuse to collect

  • No phone numbers. Not the seller’s, not the dealer’s, not a WhatsApp link.
  • No email addresses.
  • No personal names, ID numbers or logbook images.
  • No photographs. We do not copy your images onto our servers.
  • No verbatim listing copy. We extract structured facts and write our own summaries.

This is enforced in code, not policy: the extractor deletes any field whose name contains phone, mobile, whatsapp, email, seller, owner, contact or id_number before a row is ever written. We take facts about the car. To contact a seller, buyers are sent to your listing.

Kenya’s Data Protection Act 2019 requires a lawful basis for processing personal data. Scraping and republishing seller contact details has none, so we do not do it.

How we behave

Requests at once, per site
One. Always.
Delay between requests
Your robots.txt Crawl-delay, or 2 seconds
robots.txt
Obeyed. A disallowed path is never fetched.
Login walls
Never bypassed. We do not create accounts to get behind them.
Unchanged pages
Skipped via ETag / If-Modified-Since, so we cost you almost nothing
Repeated errors
We back off, then stop requesting for 15 minutes

What we publish

Aggregate statistics: median and interquartile price bands per model, year and mileage. A band is never published on fewer than three comparable listings — if we do not have the data, we say so rather than guess.

We do not mirror your catalogue, and we never present another site’s stock as our own. Listings collected by the crawler are marked Unverified and cannot carry a trust badge; a badge requires a person from our team to have visited the yard, matched an ID to a logbook, or taken the photographs.

We would rather have a feed than a crawler

If you are a dealer or a marketplace, a direct feed is better for both of us: your data arrives accurate and current, you control what we show, and we stop hitting your server. It is free, and it comes with a dealer profile.
