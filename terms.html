What this site is

An information and comparison tool. We are not a dealer, a broker, a lender, an insurer or a financial adviser. We do not sell cars, hold stock, or take payment for vehicles.

The estimates are estimates

Running costs, price bands, repayment figures, insurance premiums and import duty are calculated from published market data using stated assumptions. They are there to help you compare options and ask better questions.

They are not quotes. Fuel prices change monthly, lender rates move with the CBK rate, KRA applies its own CRSP schedule and depreciation table, and insurers price on your individual risk. Confirm every figure in writing with the provider before you commit money.

Trust labels

Verified means a person from our team physically confirmed the yard or matched the seller’s ID to the logbook. Inspected means a documented mechanical inspection exists. Unverified means we found the listing publicly and have checked nothing about it.

No label — including Verified — is a guarantee of a vehicle’s condition, title or legality. Always run the registration through NTSA TIMS and pay for your own independent inspection before any money moves.

Third-party listings and links

Where we show a listing found elsewhere we link to the source and do not act as a party to any transaction. Any dealing you have with a seller is between you and them.
