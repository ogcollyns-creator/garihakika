Questions buyers actually ask about the Subaru Impreza

Is an Impreza practical?

It has genuine symmetrical AWD and handles wet murram better than almost anything at the price. You pay for that in fuel at 10–12km/l and in servicing that needs a Subaru-literate mechanic.

Turbo or non-turbo?

For an ordinary owner, non-turbo. Turbo cars in the Kenyan used market have usually been driven hard and serviced optimistically.

Is it cheap to own?

No. Budget roughly double an equivalent Toyota for parts and servicing. Buy it because you want it, not because it looks cheap.

Import rules that affect this car

From 1 January 2026 only right-hand-drive vehicles first registered on or after 2019-01-01 can be cleared into Kenya, under KS 1515 eight-year rule. Older Subaru Imprezas are only available as locally used units already registered here — which is why that supply is fixed and slowly shrinking.
