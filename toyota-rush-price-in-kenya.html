What to check before you buy a Toyota Rush

  • Firm ride on rough roads
  • Rear axle noise on high mileage
  • Basic sound insulation

Non-negotiable, whatever the model: search the registration on tims.ntsa.go.ke to confirm the owner and check for a logbook charge, and pay for an independent inspection you arrange yourself. Those two steps cost under KSh 10,000 and prevent almost every loss in this market. Run our seller risk check.

Questions buyers actually ask about the Toyota Rush

Is the Rush a real SUV?

It has body-on-frame construction and good ground clearance, so it handles murram and rough roads far better than a crossover. It is not a serious 4x4 and most units sold are 2WD — check which you are buying.

Rush or a used Harrier for the same money?

Rush gives you a newer car, seven seats and cheap running costs. Harrier gives you comfort, refinement and status in an older vehicle. For rough roads and low running costs, the Rush is the more rational buy.

Import rules that affect this car

From 1 January 2026 only right-hand-drive vehicles first registered on or after 2019-01-01 can be cleared into Kenya, under KS 1515 eight-year rule. Older Toyota Rushs are only available as locally used units already registered here — which is why that supply is fixed and slowly shrinking.
