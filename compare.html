What we do that they mostly do not

We price the whole cost, not the sticker

Every car shows what it costs to own — fuel at the current EPRA price, insurance at market rates, servicing, tyres. A KSh 700,000 car with KSh 30,000 a month in running costs is not a cheap car, and we are the only Kenyan site that says so on the card.

We tell you whether the asking price is fair

Prices are compared against a band built from listings across the market, so you know before you call whether you are being quoted sensibly. When we do not have enough data for a band, we say that instead of guessing.

We refuse to publish thin data

A price band needs at least three comparable listings before it appears. Most sites would show you a number anyway.

We answer the questions people actually ask

71 models with known faults, parts availability, resale strength and the specific questions buyers ask — the N20 timing chain, the Nissan CVT, what a hybrid battery really costs to replace in Nairobi.

We show you the whole financing picture

Bank rates against non-bank lenders, with total cost of credit rather than a monthly figure. The gap between a 14% bank loan and a 35% effective non-bank rate is the single most expensive decision most buyers make.

We help you check the seller

An eight-point fraud checklist and a seller risk tool built on the patterns that actually cost Kenyans money, pointing you at NTSA TIMS and an independent inspection.

What we are worse at, today

This section exists because a comparison page without one is marketing. Everything below is true as at 2026-07-27.

We have no live inventory yet

Live listings are switched off. The cars you can browse today are illustrative. Peach Cars, Jiji and PigiaMe all have real stock right now and we do not.

We have not inspected a single car

No trust badge on this site has been earned by anyone from our team visiting a yard. When listings go live they will be marked Unverified until that changes.

Our price bands are not built yet

They need real listing data across the market. Until that exists we show asking prices without a fair-price verdict.

Our coverage is thin outside Nairobi and Mombasa

So is everyone's — but we are not going to pretend that is a strength. 35 of Kenya's 47 counties have no dealer on our list yet.

We cannot sell you a car

We are not a dealer and we take no part in the transaction. If you want someone to hand you keys and a warranty, a franchise dealer or Peach Cars is the right call.
